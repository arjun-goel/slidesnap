How SlideSnap's De-Occlusion Pipeline Works

1

Scene Regime Clustering: Computes 64-bit perceptual dHash + fast SSIM to isolate stable slide intervals and reject cross-dissolve transitions.

2

Presenter & Blur Ranking: Segments the teacher's silhouette/skin/motion mask and scores background Laplacian sharpness to pick the clearest unobstructed frame.

3

Temporal Median Reconstruction: If the teacher never leaves the slide, SlideSnap aligns frames as they walk and fuses only unobstructed background pixels, labeling reconstructed slides in the PDF.
